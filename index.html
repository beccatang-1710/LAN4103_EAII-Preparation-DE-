Task 1: Presenting Statistical Data

任务一：呈现统计数据

Quantitative findings are the numerical data collected using tools such as questionnaire surveys. They are expressed in numbers, percentages, or statistics, and provide measurable evidence to assess a project's effectiveness objectively. Visuals help people see comparisons, relationships, or trends clearly.

量化研究结果是使用问卷调查等工具收集的数值数据，以数字、百分比或统计数据表示，提供可量化的证据以客观评估项目成效。视觉图表有助于人们清晰地看到比较、关系或趋势。
Vocabulary: objectively (adverb) — considering only facts and without being influenced by personal feelings or opinions

Match the Visual Type to Its Purpose

Task 2a — Describing Distributions

When describing a pie chart or percentage distribution, you can combine numerical descriptions (e.g., 60%) with general descriptions (e.g., 'more than half of the respondents').

描述饼图或百分比分布时，可将数字描述（如60%）与概括描述（如「超过半数受访者」）结合使用。
Sample Formula: [General description] + [Segment description] + [Conjunction] + [Numerical description] + [Segment description]
Example: "More than half of the respondents repurposed their old clothes regularly, whereas 30% of them did so occasionally."

Numerical Descriptions

  • (more/less than) 5% · one-tenth / one-fifth / a quarter
  • one-third / two-thirds / three-quarters

General Descriptions

  • (more/less than) half of · a majority of / a minority of
  • (very) few of / almost all / all / none of
Reporting Grammatical Adjustments:
• 'you' → 'respondents' / 'participants'
• 'repurpose' → 'repurposed' (simple past tense for completed surveys)
• 'your' → 'their'
• 'did so' to avoid repeating the previous verb phrase

Task 3: Reporting Salient Findings

It is not necessary to report every detail. Select only the most salient findings to keep your evaluation focused and impactful. Highlight key changes such as a significant rise or decline, or major contrasts that demonstrate project impact.

Learning Objectives

学习目标

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

  • analyse qualitative data from surveys, interviews, and focus groups

  • report negative comments tactfully using professional softening techniques
学完本单元后，你将能够：分析来自调查、访谈和焦点小组的质性数据；报告正面评论并提取主题洞察；运用专业缓和技巧巧妙地报告负面评论。

Introduction

While quantitative data provides measurable evidence of a project's outcomes, qualitative data (opinions, open-ended responses, interviews) reveals the reasons and insights behind the figures. You will learn to report qualitative findings to complement data-driven results with human perspectives.

量化数据提供项目成果的可量化证据，而质性数据（意见、开放式回答、访谈）则揭示数据背后的原因和洞察。你将学习报告质性研究结果，以人文视角补充数据驱动的结果。
Vocabulary: qualitative (adjective) — relating to what something is like or how good it is, rather than how much of it there is

Task 3: Reporting Negative Comments Tactfully (1/3)

任务三：巧妙地报告负面评论

To provide a balanced evaluation, negative comments must be reported to pinpoint areas for improvement. Use neutral, professional, and constructive language rather than harsh or critical wording.

为提供平衡的评估，必须报告负面评论以指出改进领域。使用中立、专业和建设性的语言，而非严厉或批评性的措辞。

Comparison

Harsh: "The hand-sewing techniques are so complex. The useless instructions make it impossible to follow."

Version A (Direct): "Some expressed that the hand-sewing techniques were too complex and they could not follow the instructions."

Version B (Tactful & Constructive): "Some thought the demonstration session could be more efficient if it included clearer instructions and more hands-on practice."

Task 3: Reporting Negative Comments Tactfully (2/3)

任务三：巧妙地报告负面评论

Techniques for Tactful Reporting

TechniqueDirect / Rude FeedbackTactful Reporting
1. Replace rude/offensive words with neutral terms 'The consultation fees are a complete RIP-OFF!' Some clients thought that the consultation fees were somewhat higher than expected.
2. Focus on the issue, not the person 'The designer spoke so fast; I couldn't understand her ideas.' Feedback indicated that the presentation pace was rather fast, making explanations occasionally difficult to follow.
3. Use frequency words to show non-constant issues 'If I can't book an appointment through the online system, what's the point?' It was reported that the online booking system occasionally experienced technical issues.
4. Highlight constructive elements 'The wait time was ridiculous! Took over a week to get a response!' Feedback revealed that client response times could be improved to enhance service efficiency.

2. Methodology 研究方法

2.2 Implementation 实施

From July to August 202-, daily VR-guided tours with audio guides in Cantonese, Putonghua, and English were conducted to accommodate a diverse range of visitors. These tours allowed them to explore 3D virtual environments introducing the cultural context and creative process behind each art piece before they engaged with the physical exhibits. Each session began with a brief demonstration by facilitators to explain the use of VR tools and highlight the connection between the virtual experience and the physical exhibits. Technical support was provided when necessary.

202-年7月至8月，每日举办设有粤语、普通话及英语语音导赏的VR导赏团，以迎合不同背景的参观者。这些导赏团让他们在接触实体展品之前，先探索3D虚拟环境，了解每件艺术品背后的文化背景及创作过程。每节导赏开始前，协助员先作简短示范，讲解VR工具的使用方法，并强调虚拟体验与实体展品之间的联系。有需要时提供技术支援。
